## Measurable goals

- Improve information comprehension through bilingual storyboard completion and four-question comprehension checks.
- Make voluntary choice, consent, payment-proof completion, correction turnaround, reviewer turnaround, and upload reliability observable without recipient identity in analytics.
- Target WCAG 2.2 AA for keyboard, focus, labels, contrast, status announcements, reduced motion, and chart alternatives.
- Target representative mobile LCP <2.5s, CLS <0.1, and documented API percentile budgets.
- Preserve privacy: no sponsor identity access, no cross-case member access, private receipt objects, short-lived URLs, and no synthetic bypass in production.
